# relinker

## change [BLSI, BLSMSK and BLSR lowering]

- [BLSI, BLSMSK and BLSR lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/BlsLowering.cpp) uses scalar LEA, AND, XOR, NEG and OR after a TEST on the source, with two scratch registers saved below the red zone, under `--to-rosetta`, following their SDM entries: SF follows the result, ZF follows the result for BLSI and BLSR and clears for BLSMSK, CF is set for a zero source (BLSR, BLSMSK) or a nonzero source (BLSI) through STC on the branch that needs it, OF clears, and AF and PF are undefined. A 64-bit stack-relative displacement that exceeds signed disp32 after reserving the spill frame throws, as for ANDN. The execution test runs only on an x86-64 host and checks the SDM semantics, not the hardware instruction; precise fault delivery inside a spill sequence has not been verified.

## change [SHLX, SHRX, SARX and RORX lowering]

- [SHLX, SHRX, SARX and RORX lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/ShiftLowering.cpp) uses `shl`, `shr`, `sar` by CL and `ror` by immediate under `--to-rosetta`; the count is masked to the operand size by both the BMI2 instruction and the scalar one. These four leave RFLAGS unchanged where the scalar instructions write them, so the stub saves the flags with `pushfq` into a slot of a 160-byte frame below the red zone and restores them with `popfq` after the result is written; RCX is spilled to carry the count. A 64-bit stack-relative displacement that exceeds signed disp32 after reserving the frame throws, as for ANDN. The execution test runs natively on x86-64 or under Rosetta and checks the result against the SDM semantics and the flags against the values loaded before the stub, not against the hardware instruction; precise fault delivery inside a spill sequence has not been verified.

## change [BZHI and BEXTR lowering]

- [BZHI and BEXTR lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/BitfieldLowering.cpp) builds the mask with `shl` by CL and `not` on a scratch register and applies it with `and` under `--to-rosetta`, with RCX spilled in a 160-byte frame below the red zone. An index or start at or past the operand size takes the SDM's unmasked (BZHI) or zero (BEXTR) branch, not a scalar shift's masked count. BZHI sets CF through STC there, and ZF and SF follow the result; BEXTR clears CF and OF, ZF follows the result, and its SF, undefined in the SDM, follows the result here. A 64-bit stack-relative displacement that exceeds signed disp32 after reserving the frame throws, as for ANDN. The execution test runs natively on x86-64 or under Rosetta and checks result and flags against an SDM model that matched a Zen 2's BZHI and BEXTR on 122,880 cases, not against the hardware instruction in the same run; precise fault delivery inside a spill sequence has not been verified.

## add Functional

- [PDEP and PEXT lowering](../../../core/relinker/codegen/src/x86/BitfieldLowering.cpp) - the BMI2 deposit (PDEP) and extract (PEXT) instructions are lowered under `--to-rosetta` without the hardware instruction. PDEP walks the set mask bits in ascending order with a negate, an AND, a test, a shift and a clear per bit; PEXT walks them from the highest set mask bit with BSR, BT, ADC and BTR per bit. A mask of all ones costs 64 iterations, and both operations use four scratch registers and four frame slots. ZF follows the result, CF and OF clear, and SF, AF and PF are undefined as in the SDM. A 64-bit stack-relative displacement that exceeds signed disp32 after reserving the spill frame throws, as for BZHI. The execution test checks result and flags against an SDM model; the lowering also matched the hardware `PDEP` and `PEXT` on an Intel Core 5 210H for 100,072 edge and random pairs per operation, with no mismatch. No title run has been compared, and precise fault delivery inside the spill sequence has not been verified.
